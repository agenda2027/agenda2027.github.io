Method and corrections

Every post here wears a label. This page explains what each one means, where our facts come from, and how we fix mistakes.

The labels

LabelWhat it means
AnalysisDocumented facts, each tied to a named source: official records, declassified files, court or parliamentary reports, peer-reviewed studies, or established news outlets.
Fact checkA popular claim set against the record. We say what holds, what does not, and what nobody can know yet.
NewsRecent reporting, with the outlet and date.
MysteryA real, documented event that has no settled explanation. The known facts come first; the theories are named as theories.
ScienceWhat studies and agencies report, including their uncertainty. Not prophecy.
ForecastProjections made by researchers or institutions, and sometimes our own. A forecast is not a fact, and we say whose it is.
TheorySpeculation, ours or widely shared, clearly marked. We point out where the theory breaks or lacks evidence.

How we source

  • Every video and carousel lists its sources in the caption and on its page here.
  • We prefer primary records: declassified documents, official reports, studies. When we use news reporting, we name the outlet and the date.
  • We do not invent quotes, numbers or events. When a figure is an estimate, we say so and give the range.
  • When the evidence is mixed, we show both sides and say which one is better supported.

How the videos are made

  • Narration uses a synthetic voice.
  • Documents shown on screen are illustrative reconstructions, not the original files, and are marked that way. Text on them is paraphrased or summarized unless shown in quotation marks.
  • Charts and numbers on screen come from the sources listed for that video.

Independence

Agenda.2027 is an independent project. We are not affiliated with The Economist or any other publication, government or company we write about.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix the page and add a note here with the date and what changed. To report an error, use the error report form (no name or email needed), or check the Ledger and send a message to @agenda2027.daily with the source that shows it.

Forecasts we made or repeated are tracked on the Scorecard, with the date we will check them. Reader theories on the Open File are reviewed before they appear and labeled as unverified.
