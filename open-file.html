What gets published

  • Theories about events, institutions, technology and history.
  • Comments that add evidence, questions or counterpoints.

What does not

  • Accusations against private individuals, or crimes pinned on named people without a court record.
  • Personal data, threats, harassment, hate or spam.
  • Medical advice or anything that could put someone in danger.
  • Copied text from other sites.

Review usually takes a few days. We may shorten a post or add a short note with known facts. Published posts are anonymous; we do not ask for your email.

Agenda.2027 decodes the stories shaping the next few years. Analysis posts name their sources. Theory posts separate fact from speculation and say where each theory breaks.
