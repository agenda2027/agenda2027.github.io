The Somerton Man

Sealed fileThe Somerton ManWatch on @agenda2027.daily

A dead man, no name, and a code nobody has cracked since 1948.

Watch it on @agenda2027.daily

December 1st, 1948. A man is found dead on Somerton Beach, in Adelaide, Australia.

Well dressed. No ID. The labels cut out of his clothes.

In a hidden pocket, a scrap of paper: Tamam Shud. It means ended, or finished.

It was torn from a book of Persian poems. In the back of that book, a code nobody has ever cracked.

Soviet spy? Jilted lover? For seventy four years, nobody even knew his name.

In 2022, researchers used DNA from his hair to name him: Carl Webb, an engineer from Melbourne.

Police and the coroner still have not confirmed it. And how he died is still unknown.
