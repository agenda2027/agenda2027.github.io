Why agents raided the game maker

In text

March 1, 1990

Armed Secret Service agents raided Steve Jackson Games in Austin, Texas.

The rumor

Theorists claim the raid tried to stop the Illuminati game from revealing a real plan.

The reality

The raid was part of a hacker investigation. Agents took computers and the company's 'Illuminati' message board.

The verdict

A federal court ruled the raid careless and unjustified, and awarded damages to the company.
