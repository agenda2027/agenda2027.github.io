Not a pandemic 💉

A syringe on the 2027 cover. Another pandemic? The real story is your grocery bill.

Cornell tracked 150,000 households on weight-loss drugs: grocery spending down 5.3% in six months, fast food down 8%, snacks down 10%+.

But about a third of users stop.
