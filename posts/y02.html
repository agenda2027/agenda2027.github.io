1971: burglars broke into the FBI. What they found was worse than the crime.

Watch it on @agenda2027.daily

On March 8th, 1971, eight activists broke into an FBI office in Media, Pennsylvania.

They took more than a thousand documents, and mailed copies to newspapers.

One file carried a strange word: COINTELPRO.

It was a secret FBI program, from 1956 to 1971, to discredit and neutralize American groups it saw as subversive.

Civil rights leaders, anti-war groups, the Black Panthers. Informants, fake letters, smear campaigns, even against Martin Luther King.

The burglars were never caught. In 2014, some of them finally revealed their names.

Without that break-in, would we ever have known?
