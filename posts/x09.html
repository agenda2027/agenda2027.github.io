The Voynich manuscript

Sealed fileThe Voynich manuscriptWatch on @agenda2027.daily

A 600-year-old book nobody can read.

Watch it on @agenda2027.daily

There is a book that nobody in the world can read.

Around two hundred and forty pages, in an alphabet that appears nowhere else.

Strange plants, star charts, figures bathing in green pools.

Radiocarbon dating puts its parchment between 1404 and 1438.

Wartime codebreakers tried. Linguists and computers have tried. No proposed solution has been accepted.

A lost language, a cipher, or a six hundred year old hoax? All three are still on the table.

You can see every page online today. You just can't understand it.

Sources named in the video

  • Yale University, Beinecke Rare Book & Manuscript Library, MS 408 (digitized 2020)
  • University of Arizona radiocarbon dating (announced Feb 2011)
