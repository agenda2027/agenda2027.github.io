The debt trap in 4 numbers

In text

$49T to $111T

Global public debt: $49 trillion in 2010, $111 trillion in 2025.

119%

France's public debt as a share of its GDP.

Yields up

Bond yields have risen in Britain, France, Germany and Japan. Higher rates make big debts more expensive.

The counterpoint

Moody's still expects steady growth in 2027-28, in a high-risk environment. A risk, not a prophecy.
