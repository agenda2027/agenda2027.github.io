The Wow! signal

Sealed fileThe Wow! signalWatch on @agenda2027.daily

72 seconds from space. Never heard again.

Watch it on @agenda2027.daily

August 1977. A radio telescope in Ohio caught something.

A signal so strong and so narrow, the astronomer circled it on the printout and wrote one word. Wow.

It lasted seventy two seconds, right next to the frequency of hydrogen, where scientists expected aliens to call.

It has never been detected again.

In 2024, astronomers proposed a natural cause: a stellar flare suddenly lighting up a cold hydrogen cloud.

But that is still a hypothesis. Nobody has proven what it was.

Forty nine years later, the sky has not repeated itself.
