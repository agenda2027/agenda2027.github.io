China's silent drills

In text

No name, no warning

In early November, China ran a large military exercise around Taiwan and the western Pacific without announcing it.

100+ vessels

A Taiwanese security official said China's military and coast guard massed more than 100 vessels at one point.

'Area denial'

Taiwan's defence minister said the Liaoning carrier group ran area-denial drills in the western Pacific.

The counterpoint

Few officials or analysts predict immediate military action. The assessments do not show Beijing has decided on a blockade.
