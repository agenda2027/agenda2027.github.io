AI 2027: the deadline is back

Sealed fileAI 2027: the deadline is backWatch on @agenda2027.daily

The AI 2027 authors just moved the deadline back to 2027.

Watch it on @agenda2027.daily

In April 2025, a group of researchers published a forecast called AI 2027.

Their scenario: by 2027, AI could do the work of the best AI researchers, and start improving itself.

Critics called it science fiction. In early 2026, even the authors pushed their own estimates back.

Then, on November 10th, 2026, they changed course again.

In their new update, all of their forecasters expect a superhuman AI researcher sometime in 2027.

It is a forecast, not a fact. Many experts think it is far too early.

But the people who wrote it think the deadline is next year.
