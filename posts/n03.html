Is 2027 already the hottest year?

In text

79%+

NOAA (Nov 12) gives a greater than 79% chance of a strong-to-very-strong El Niño through Jan-Mar 2027.

95%

A Berkeley Earth analysis puts a 95% chance on 2027 becoming the hottest year on record.

Peak: December

Forecasters expect the El Niño to peak around the end of 2026, with effects well into 2027.

The fine print

NOAA does not officially use the word 'super'. A forecast is a probability, not a certainty.
