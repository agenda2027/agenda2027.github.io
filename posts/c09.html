Who really owns The Economist?

In text

The largest shareholder

Exor, the Agnelli family holding behind Fiat, owns about 43.4% of The Economist Group.

The Rothschild stake

In March 2026 the Rothschild family sold its 26.9% to Canadian billionaire Stephen Smith.

The 20% cap

No shareholder can control more than 20% of the votes.

Trustees

Independent trustees must approve the editor, a structure that dates back to 1928.
