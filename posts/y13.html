November 2026. Americans just voted in the first election where AI was built into every major social platform.

Campaigns spent more than eighty million dollars on over a hundred and sixty AI-made ads.

Researchers found only about one in four carried an AI label.

One day after early voting began in Texas, a deepfake falsely accused a Senate candidate of a crime. Researchers linked it to a Russian network within twenty four hours.

Did deepfakes change any results? So far, researchers can't show that they did.

Our forecast: by 2028, the danger won't only be fake videos. It will be people refusing to believe real ones.
