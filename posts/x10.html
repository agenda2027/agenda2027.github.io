The Antikythera mechanism

Sealed fileThe Antikythera mechanismWatch on @agenda2027.daily

A computer from 2,000 years ago. It predicted eclipses.

Watch it on @agenda2027.daily

In 1901, sponge divers pulled a lump of corroded bronze from an ancient shipwreck.

Decades later, X rays revealed what was hiding inside. Gears. Dozens of them.

It is around two thousand years old, and it could predict eclipses.

It tracked the sun, the moon, and even the cycle of the Olympic Games.

Nothing this complex appears again for more than a thousand years.

Who built it is unknown. One hypothesis points to the school of Archimedes.

If this was possible two thousand years ago, what else did history forget?
