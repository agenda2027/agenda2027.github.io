The AMOC

Sealed fileThe AMOCWatch on @agenda2027.daily

The current that keeps Europe warm is weakening. New study: about half by 2100.

Watch it on @agenda2027.daily

There is an ocean current that keeps Europe warm. It's called the AMOC.

It carries warm water north, and cold water back south.

And it is getting weaker.

In April 2026, a study in Science Advances estimated it could slow by around half by 2100.

The researchers say a slowdown that large would very likely end in collapse.

That could mean harsher winters in Europe, shifting rains in the tropics, and higher seas on American coasts.

The UN climate panel says collapse before 2100 is unlikely. These scientists are not so sure.

Sources named in the video

  • Portmann et al., Science Advances (15 Apr 2026): AMOC projected to slow 42 to 58% by 2100 (reported by The Guardian, 15 Apr 2026)
  • IPCC AR6 WG1 (2021): AMOC decline very likely; abrupt collapse before 2100 judged unlikely (medium confidence)
  • Carbon Brief: Is global warming tipping key Atlantic ocean currents towards collapse?
