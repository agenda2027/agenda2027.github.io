The 2026 cover: rumor vs reality

In text

The rumor

Conspiracy blogs called the 2026 cover 'super creepy', saying it showed war, pestilence and financial collapse.

What it said

The issue itself said wars would grind on in Ukraine, Sudan and Myanmar, and flagged a growing bond-market risk.

What happened

On Feb 28, 2026, the US and Israel attacked Iran. That war was not one of the issue's headline themes.

The pattern

War covers appear when the world is already at war. Read the articles, not just the drawing.
