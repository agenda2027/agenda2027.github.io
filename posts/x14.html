53% of web traffic is no longer human.

Watch it on @agenda2027.daily

What if most of the internet isn't human anymore?

That's the Dead Internet theory. And part of it can now be measured.

In 2024, bots made up fifty one percent of web traffic. In 2025, fifty three percent, according to Imperva.

That's traffic, not people writing posts. But the line keeps going up.

Our theory, and it is only a theory: the next internet won't be bots pretending to be human. It will be AI talking to AI, while humans watch.

So ask yourself: who wrote the last thing that changed your mind?
