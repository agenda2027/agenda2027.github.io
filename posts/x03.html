Gulf of Tonkin

The attack that started a war never happened.

Watch it on @agenda2027.daily

On August 4th, 1964, American destroyers reported a second attack in the Gulf of Tonkin.

Within days, Congress gave the president the power to go to war in Vietnam.

The first attack, on August 2nd, was real.

The second one never happened.

In 2005, the NSA declassified its own historian's study. No enemy boats were there that night. Only radar ghosts and misread intercepts.

The resolution that opened the war rested partly on an attack that did not exist.
