Friday the 13th, 2029: the god of chaos skims Earth.

Watch it on @agenda2027.daily

Friday, April 13th, 2029. An asteroid named after the Egyptian god of chaos will skim past the Earth.

Apophis is about as big as the Empire State Building is tall.

It will pass within thirty two thousand kilometers. Closer than our geostationary satellites.

When it was discovered in 2004, the chance of impact in 2029 rose to 2.7 percent. The highest risk rating ever recorded.

Today, radar observations rule out any impact for at least a hundred years.

That night, it may be visible to the naked eye from parts of Europe, Africa and Asia.

A god of chaos, on Friday the 13th. A coincidence, not a prophecy. But what an omen.
