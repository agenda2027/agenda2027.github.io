Hormuz: the 2026 timeline

In text

Feb 28

The US and Israel attack Iran. Iran answers by choking the Strait of Hormuz, where about a fifth of the world's oil passes.

April

Oil heads toward $100 a barrel. A ceasefire follows.

July

The ceasefire breaks down and Hormuz is disrupted again.

November

Iran says Hormuz stays shut until its demands are met. Talks continue through Qatar.
