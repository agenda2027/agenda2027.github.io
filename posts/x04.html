For more than twenty years, the U.S. government paid psychics to spy.

It was called Stargate. From 1972 to 1995, around twenty million dollars.

The method was remote viewing. Describing a hidden target using only the mind.

Targets included training camps in Libya, Soviet submarines, and kidnapped hostages.

In 1995, an independent review for the CIA found it never produced information that was useful in an operation.

Fact: the program was real. Theory: that it worked.

So why did it survive for two decades?
