The Illuminati Cards

FACT CHECKNov 29, 2026

The card that 'predicted' 9/11

In text

The game

Illuminati: New World Order, a satirical card game by Steve Jackson Games, released in 1994.

'Terrorist Nuke'

One card shows an explosion in a skyscraper, drawn in 1994, seven years before 9/11.

The artist's answer

Illustrator John Grigni said it reflected post-Soviet terrorism fears, citing Hamas. Not foresight.

Context

The World Trade Center had already been bombed in 1993, a year before the card was drawn.
