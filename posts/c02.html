Why 2027? Three dates

In text

March 2021

US Admiral Philip Davidson tells the Senate the threat to Taiwan could come within the next six years. Six years from 2021 is 2027.

February 2023

CIA director William Burns says Xi told his army to be ready by 2027. He adds: ready doesn't mean decided.

2027

The People's Liberation Army turns 100.

The counterpoint

In a 2022 CSIS survey, 83% of experts said China did not plan major force against Taiwan by 2027.
